# one-way-loop-c -- HARD variant of "One-Way Loop (asymmetric circuit)".
#
# THE RING. A solid island (cols 8-23, rows 5-13) with corridor all the way
# round it: top run rows 1-4, bottom run rows 14-16, ascent shaft cols 4-7,
# descent shaft cols 24-27. Three doors hang off the ring -- ceiling onto the
# top run, west and floor onto the bottom run. Every way DOWN is free. Every
# way UP is either gloves or blood. That asymmetry is the whole room; the
# player learns it as a map, not as a sequence of jumps.
#
# HARD EXPRESSION -- three of the class's variant axes stacked, not a scale-up:
#
#  1. THE ISLAND IS HOLLOW, so the ring is a figure-eight. Two one-way tiles in
#     the island's floor (row 13, cols 14-15 and 18-19) let you jump UP into
#     the vault from the bottom run and then stand on them. The right-hand pair
#     sits under a chimney (cols 18-19) that runs clean through the island to
#     the top run: from above it is a free drop into the vault, from below it
#     is a gloves-only wall-jump between the pillar at col 17 and the face at
#     col 22. So the inside of the loop is a third route that CROSSES the ring
#     instead of circling it, and it too runs one way for free and the other
#     way only with gloves.
#
#  2. THE DESCENT SIDE IS A CONTROLLED FALL WITH CONSEQUENCES. The right shaft
#     has two one-way catches (rows 7 and 12) five tiles apart -- comfortable
#     to fall through, 50px to climb, out of jump range. Each catch carries a
#     ^ spike on the OPPOSITE edge (col 24 on the upper, col 27 on the lower),
#     so the fall has to zig-zag, and a shutter fires in the gap between them:
#     the drop has to be entered on a beat. Boots can force this shaft back
#     upwards -- deliberately, since jump+up-dash clears 50px -- but they pay
#     the spikes and the shutter for it. That is the class's "a blind re-climb
#     costs blood", and it is why the room is hard rather than merely gated.
#
#  3. THE ASCENT SIDE IS BARE AND HONEST. The left shaft is twelve tiles of
#     rock, two faces four tiles apart, no platforms, no hazards, no spikes:
#     gloves or nothing, and falling down it is completely free. The room reads
#     as a shortcut on the way down and a wall on the way back up.
#
# The whole outer wall of the descent shaft (col 28, rows 6-12) is a column of
# < spikes pointing into the shaft (row 12 is solid behind the lower catch
# spike): their backs block movement but give no wall surface, so the descent
# shaft cannot be wall-jumped even though it is four tiles wide. The gating is
# geometric and visible from either end.
#
# No dead space: both top-run pockets (cols 1-3, cols 28-30) are ledges across
# a shaft mouth that you can miss, the bottom run is the safe closing arc that
# makes the loop a loop, the block at cols 18-21 row 16 is the launcher into
# the chimney, and the island interior is the vault.
door west
door ceiling
door floor
