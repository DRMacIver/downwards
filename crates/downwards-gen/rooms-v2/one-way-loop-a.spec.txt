# one-way-loop-a -- easy variant of "One-Way Loop (asymmetric circuit)".
#
# A ring of corridor around a solid island (cols 9-24, rows 5-13). The ring has
# four arcs: the top run (rows 1-4), the bottom run (rows 14-16), the LEFT arc
# (cols 1-8) and the RIGHT arc (cols 25-30). The two vertical arcs are the whole
# point, because they are different problems:
#
#   LEFT ARC -- a zigzag staircase of one-way rungs at rows 15, 13, 11, 9, 7,
#   alternating between the col 1-4 and col 5-8 bands. Every step is a 20px rise
#   with a landing you can stand still on, and the top rung sits flush against the
#   island's left face, so the stair hands you straight onto the top run. Anyone
#   can climb it with no abilities at all; pressing down walks it back the other
#   way. This is the ring's slow, safe, reversible side.
#
#   RIGHT ARC -- a 6-tile-wide shaft with exactly ONE landing: a one-way slab at
#   row 10 spanning the shaft's full width. Coming down, you step off the island's
#   right edge, fall 50px onto the slab, walk it, and drop through for a 70px fall
#   to the bottom run. Coming up, both of those drops are far past a 40px jump,
#   and the shaft is 60px wide so there is no facing wall to chain off. The right
#   arc is a genuine one-way street at EVERY loadout, gloves and boots included.
#
# THE TWIST, made easy: the class gates the ascent behind wall-jump or dash. This
# variant gates it behind GEOMETRY instead. Nothing here needs wall or dash -- the
# entire lap is walkable at loadout NONE -- and yet the lap still only runs one
# way round, because the fast side is a chute and the slow side is a stair. The
# player learns the room as a map ("down the right, up the left") rather than as a
# sequence of jumps, which is the class's real verb, without ever being asked for
# a hard input. That is what makes this the "a" variant: the asymmetry is fully
# legible and completely free, so the room teaches the shape before a later
# variant charges for it.

door west
door ceiling

# The two doors sit at opposite ends of the ring: the west mouth (rows 13-16)
# opens at the foot of the left stair, the ceiling mouth (cols 14-17) drops onto
# the row-3 rung floating over the island. Every ordered pair closes at NONE:
#   west -> ceiling : up the left stair, east across the island, hop the row-3
#                     rung, out through the roof.
#   ceiling -> west : east across the island, off the edge into the chute, then
#                     west along the bottom run.
# The self-pairs close in a few ticks each, so a player bounced off a sealed door
# can always retreat. Note that the return trip is not the outbound trip reversed
# -- that asymmetry is the entire class, and here it costs nothing to use.

# COIN 1 -- the lap-closer, on the bottom run under the middle of the island. The
# bottom arc is the flat safe segment that guarantees the loop always closes, and
# this coin makes it a place you visit rather than a gutter.
coin 180 160

# COIN 2 -- the twist coin, and the reason this room is a loop rather than a
# corridor. It rests on the row-10 slab inside the right shaft. You can see it
# from the bottom run and you cannot reach it from there: 70px of blank shaft with
# no wall on either side. The only approach is to fall onto the slab from the
# island above. A player entering by the WEST door, fifteen tiles away in a
# straight line, must climb the whole left stair, cross the island and commit to
# the chute -- one full lap -- to take it. That is the "shortcut down, wall coming
# back up" reading of the room turned into a single visible object.
coin 270 90

# COIN 3 -- the stair's own reward, sitting on the top rung of the left arc where
# the staircase meets the island. Cheap from either door; it marks the handoff
# point and gives the climb a destination before the top run begins.
coin 60 60

# THE BEAT -- a shutter across the full width of the right shaft, two rows below
# the slab. 180-tick period, 60 ticks closed. It gives the chute a cadence: land
# on the slab, take coin 2, then wait for the window before dropping through. This
# is the class's "timed hazard occupying one arc so the loop must be entered on a
# beat" axis, kept gentle (two thirds of the cycle is open, and the slab is a
# stand-still landing you can wait on indefinitely). It only ever punishes
# commitment -- dropping early -- never a jump that falls short.
hazard 250 120 60 20 180 60 0
