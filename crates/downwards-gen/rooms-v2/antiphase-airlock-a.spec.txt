# antiphase-airlock-a -- easy variant of "Antiphase Airlock".
#
# Three tall chambers (cols 1-9, 11-20, 22-30) separated by two full-height
# partitions (cols 10 and 21). Each partition is pierced ONLY at floor level,
# rows 14-16, and that 3-tile mouth is filled by a shutter: a narrow
# TimedHazard column. Every chamber has an unbroken floor at row 17, so every
# chamber is a bank point. Progress is "be standing in a chamber, read the
# beat, walk through". No jump, no dash, no spike: the hazard is temporal, not
# spatial, which is exactly what keeps this off the spike-hop-corridor pile.
#
# THE ANTIPHASE. Period 180, shut for 60. Gate 1 shuts at phase 120, gate 2 at
# phase 30 -- half a cycle apart, so the two gates are never shut together and
# never open-and-shut together either. From chamber 2 the player can always see
# one gate closing while the other opens. There are two 30-tick windows per
# cycle where both gates stand open: that is the "ride the wave" line, a single
# uninterrupted sprint west-to-east. Miss it and you simply bank in chamber 2
# and take the next beat. Retreat is symmetric -- the same windows run
# backwards -- so a player bounced off a locked door ahead is never trapped.
#
# The two phases are offset so that BOTH entrances get a free first gate: a
# player walking in from either door reaches the near shutter inside its open
# window without having to stop. The room introduces itself by letting you
# through, and only asks for the beat on the second gate. That is what makes
# it the easy variant in feel and not just in numbers -- measured worst-case
# jitter robustness on the no-ability route is 94-100% on both coins from both
# doors, i.e. the timing is legible, not twitchy.
door west
door east

# WHY THIS IS THE EASY VARIANT, and not a shrunken hard one.
# Axes chosen at the soft end: three chambers instead of five; wide chambers
# (9-10 tiles) so banking never means stopping on a knife edge next to a gate;
# 120 of every 180 ticks open, so the gates are mostly open and the room reads
# as breathing rather than as a lock; gates at floor level so passage is a walk.
# The door-to-door route needs NO abilities at all.
# But it is not just "the same room, slower": the easy expression puts its own
# twist on the board rather than dropping difficulty knobs. Two things:
#   (1) one coin sits INSIDE a gate mouth, which converts that gate from a
#       thing you pass into a thing you enter and back out of;
#   (2) the overhead bypass is here, and it is dash-gated by 1-tile vents, so
#       the beginner route and the expert route share the room instead of the
#       expert route being deleted for being too hard.

# COIN 0 -- chamber 2, dead centre of the middle hall, on the floor. The
# "one gate deep" coin: cheap, but you cannot have it without having read the
# beat once. Placed mid-chamber, far from either mouth, so the geometry that
# earns it is the shutter and not a reach.
coin 152 158

# COIN 1 -- THE TWIST, sitting in the mouth of gate 2 (col 21, bottom of the
# doorway), fully inside the shutter's footprint. You cannot bank on it and you
# cannot loiter for it: you step into the gate on its open beat and either
# carry on east or back out west, and the antiphase means whichever way you
# leave, the other gate's state has already changed. It turns the room's one
# purely passive object -- a doorway -- into the thing you have to time.
coin 212 158

# COIN 2 -- BONUS, on the overhead express lane above chamber 2, which is the
# one stretch of lane sealed off from the halls below. The lane (solid floor at
# row 5) crosses both partitions through 1-tile vents at row 4: 10px of
# clearance against a 12px player, so it is a dash-crawl, and 40px of jump
# cannot open it. Reaching the lane at all is a three-rung one-way ladder in
# chamber 1 or 3 -- climbable with no abilities, so nobody gets stranded up
# there -- but crossing it needs dash. That makes the lane an untimed express
# route past both shutters, paid for in ability rather than in rhythm, and
# because it has no way down into chamber 2 it cannot bank: the class's
# "overhead route cannot rest" rule, enforced by the wall rather than by
# spikes.
coin 152 38
