# Orrery Hub: the four-way crossing where two planets keep opposite time.
#
# A full crossroads (all four doors) that stays kind to through-traffic
# because dungeon routes will cross it again and again. The ground
# floor is one straight walk west-east over a one-way sill bridging
# the floor mouth (row 16, cols 14-17): walk across it, or press down
# mid-bridge to take the floor door. Two "planet" pulses orbit the
# walk out of phase — a west pulse (x 30-90) and an east pulse
# (x 230-290) on the same 72-tick year, half a cycle apart — so
# crossing the room is two beats taken in rhythm, never a wall of
# fire, and the sill between them is always a safe survey stand.

# west bay's tower: a masonry step (cols 9-10) planted just clear of
# the west pulse's arc, then three rungs (rows 12/9/6) zig-zagging up
# in honest 30px hops to a final hop east onto the axle shingle.
