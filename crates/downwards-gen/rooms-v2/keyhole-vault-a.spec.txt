# Keyhole Vault - "a" (easy) variant: fall in for free, walk the circuit out.
#
# ENTRY IS ONE-WAY, BY GEOMETRY. The ceiling funnel narrows to a two-tile
# throat whose walls at rows 3-4 are side-facing spikes ('>' at col 14, '<' at
# col 17). Dropping straight through is perfectly safe; a wall-jump chain back
# up puts the 8px body onto a flank spike. No shutter, no key - the entrance
# simply cannot be reversed. That is why every "-> ceiling" pair audits as
# inconclusive at every loadout: it is the class, not a fault. Nobody is
# trapped - both side doors are reachable from the ceiling with NO abilities.
#
# THE VAULT HAS A LID. The row-10 slab roofs the chamber from col 9 to col 22
# with one three-tile keyhole at cols 14-16, sitting exactly under the throat.
# You fall through the throat, through the keyhole, and land on the coin. The
# lid is also the gallery you walk at rows 8-9, so the room's silhouette is a
# funnel over a sealed box with a slot in it.
#
# TWO PRICES OUT, as the class demands:
#  - Safe stair, no abilities: from the vault floor walk right to the steps at
#    (21,15) and (23,13), up onto the col-25 wall top, and down the east bay
#    (29,15)/(28,13)/(27,11)) to the east door. Cheap - if east is where you
#    wanted to go.
#  - The same stair to the WEST door is the full circuit: up the stair, up onto
#    the lid, ~20 tiles back left along the gallery hopping the mouth of the
#    keyhole you fell through, then down the left bay.
#  - Fast shaft, wall or dash: cols 6-8 between the bare faces of col 5 and
#    col 9, entered through the 2-tile gap at the foot of col 9 and topped by a
#    hole in the lid. Three moves and you are out on the left, beside the west
#    door. 60px of bare wall, so it is not a jump.
# Easy variant: the shortcut is never a gate. Bootless players are slowed, not
