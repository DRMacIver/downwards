# greed-loop-b -- medium variant of the level class "Greed Loop".
#
# THE CLASS: a closed circuit. The exit sits right next to the entrance, the
# whole room is one lap, coins sit at increasing lap-depth, and nothing is
# banked until the lap closes back onto the bank tile.
#
# THE MEDIUM TWIST (variant axes: bank position + back-loaded hazard density):
# the bank is not a floor tile you stand on -- it is the FLOOR DOOR ITSELF, a
# 4-tile hole in the bottom of the room at cols 14-17. Banking means dropping
# out of the room. It sits in plain sight a short walk east of the west door,
# under the first thing the player steps on, so the treasury is visible from the
# moment they arrive and an abort is always one button away. Every coin, by
# contrast, is out on the ring, and nothing counts until you come back to it.
#
# THE RING (one-directional in practice, run counter-clockwise):
#   1. Bottom run, eastward, rows 13-16 -- the cheap opening. The bank platform
#      is the one-way slab at row 16 cols 14-17, a raised tile directly over the
#      treasury hole: stand on it and press down and everything you carry is
#      banked. You cross it outbound with nothing and re-cross it inbound with a
#      full purse, which is the entire decision of the room in one tile.
#   2. Spike pair at cols 19-20 row 16 -- the toll to leave the shallow end.
#   3. Right-side chimney, cols 22-30, climbed on staggered one-way rungs at
#      rows 15 / 13 / 11 / 9 / 7 (alternating column bands 22-25 and 27-30),
#      every rise a comfortable 20px. Easy up, awkward down: the ladder is what
#      makes the circuit a loop and not a there-and-back.
#   4. Top run, westward, rows 1-4 on the row-5 ledge (cols 5-21) -- the long
#      return leg, and the only stretch with no floor below it to fall back to.
#   5. Return drop through the row-5 gap at cols 2-4, straight down the left
#      shaft onto the clean landing at cols 1-9, then walk right to the hole.
# The solid island at cols 10-21 rows 6-12 is the donut hole; there is no way
# through it, so the lap is genuinely a lap.
#
# HAZARDS ARE BACK-LOADED (the axis choice): the shallow half costs one spike
# hop; the deep half is where the room bites. That is what makes the second half
# of the lap the tense half and turns "one more coin" into a real decision.
door west
door floor

# and it happens while you are already carrying everything else.
coin 61 20

# so it taxes the bank, not the pickup -- an aborted lap is what it punishes.
