# Shutter Chute - "a" (easy) variant: a staged zigzag descent.
#
# The class twist made structural: each hazard band is a full-width solid slab
# pierced by exactly one four-tile aperture, and the TimedHazard rect sits in
# that aperture. So the shutter is literally the only way down, and the slab
# either side of it is the ledge you stand on to read the beat. Nothing here
# is a chimney - there is no vertical corridor, only two slabs and the
# staggered holes through them.
#
# The apertures are offset (cols 8-11 then cols 13-16) so the descent zigzags:
# enter from the ceiling, land on the upper slab under the door, walk left,
# drop through shutter A, cross the middle bay right, drop through shutter B
# straight into the floor door. Catch-ledges (row 8 shelf, row 13 nub) sit
# under the right-hand half of each aperture, so a nervous player is caught
# and re-times while a committed player who releases down the free column
# (col 8, then col 15) falls the whole way in one unbroken drop.
