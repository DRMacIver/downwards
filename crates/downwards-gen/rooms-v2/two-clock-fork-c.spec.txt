# two-clock-fork-c -- HARD variant of "Two-Clock Fork (one crossing, two honest routes)".
#
# ONE crossing (west <-> east) served by TWO complete lanes that branch out of
# the west stair and rejoin in the east stair. Neither lane is a bonus branch.
#
#   LOW LANE (rows 15-16, the floor run) -- no ability at all. Short, flat,
#     fast, and taxed by two shutters slammed into the pillar gates at
#     cols 12-13 and cols 20-21. Period 70, active 18, phases 0 and 35: the
#     gates are only jointly open for a ~17-tick slot per cycle, so the low
#     lane is a sprint you must start on the right beat, not a walk.
#   HIGH LANE (rows 5-7, the rock-top tunnel) -- pinched to a SINGLE tile
#     (10px) at cols 12-13 and cols 20-21, which only the 8px dashing profile
#     fits through. That is a PROFILE gate, not a distance gate: no jump
#     height, no run-up and no other loadout gets through, so the gating is
#     visible from the entry bay. Its clock is one long window
#     (period 157, active 80) in the lit middle span between the pinches.
#
# THE TWIST, and why this is the hard expression of the class: the two pinches
# and the two shutters ARE THE SAME TWO PILLARS, seen from above and below.
# The pillars run from row 5 down to row 14, so a player dithering in the west
# bay watching the floor shutters is staring at the exact columns that will
# pinch them in the tunnel. And 70 and 157 are coprime, so the fork's answer
# is different on every arrival: the honest read is "which pillar am I early
# for", not "which lane is better". Per the class's hard brief the high
# window is short enough that a dasher who hesitates at the first pinch has
# already lost the second and must re-run the tunnel.
#
# No dead space: the rock mass (cols 8-25, rows 9-11) is the divider that
# creates both lanes; the two staggered one-way stairs (west cols 3-7, east
# cols 22-29) are the fork's stem and its reconvergence bay; the tunnel widens
# to 3 tiles in the run-up bays either side of each pinch so the dash has
# somewhere to be aimed from; and the pillars are load-bearing in both lanes
# at once.
