# keyhole-vault-b -- medium variant of
# "Keyhole Vault (one-way-in, earn-your-way-out)".
#
# You fall into the treasure chamber for free through a spike-lined keyhole that
# geometry alone makes irreversible, then you pay for the exit. Two prices are
# on offer, and -- this is the medium variant's axis, "the safe exit leads
# somewhere different from the fast one" -- THEY LEAVE BY DIFFERENT DOORS, so
# the choice re-routes the dungeon instead of merely costing seconds.
#
# THE KEYHOLE (rows 3-8, cols 15-17). The ceiling mouth opens into a funnel bowl
# at rows 1-2 that necks down into a 30px chute lined on BOTH faces with `>` and
# `<` spikes for six unbroken rows. Falling through is free if you keep the drop
# straight; the 22px safe band is the whole descent skill. Coming back up is not
# a matter of difficulty, it is a matter of arithmetic: the chute has no
# wall-jumpable surface anywhere in its length (spike flanks give none), its
# mouth at row 9 hangs 68px over the vault floor with nothing under it, and the
# nearest wall face (col 12, rows 11-13) tops out 60px below the mouth. A jump
# plus an up-dash buys 80px of contact-free ascent; the keyhole asks for 130.
# The solver agrees: west->ceiling and east->ceiling are inconclusive at every
# loadout including `both`, while every other intended pair solves.
#
# THE VAULT (cols 13-20, floor row 17). Coin 0 sits dead centre directly under
# the chute: the reward is free, the exit is the price. That is the class's joke
# and the reason the room decides something.
#
#   SAFE STAIR -> EAST (no ability, ~110 ticks). Four 20px steps rising east off
#   the vault floor (tops at rows 16/14/12/10, cols 21-28), then a step east into
#   the cols 29-30 chute and a 70px plunge to the east door. Long, plain, and
#   completely ungated: the greedy bootless player who dropped in for the coin is
#   slowed, never trapped.
#
#   FAST CHIMNEY -> WEST (wall or dash, ~90-140 ticks). The vault floor runs west
#   under col 12 onto a plinth at cols 8-11 row 16, and from there a 40px-wide,
#   60px-tall chimney between the col 7 and col 12 faces tops out onto the attic
#   slab (cols 5-7, row 9). Cross the slab, drop the 80px west well, walk out the
#   west door. Three moves against thirty -- and it is the only way to leave on
#   the west side, so gloves or boots literally buy a different destination.
#
# Gating is loud: the chimney is 60px of smooth facing rock and the west well is
# 80px, both far past the 40px jump, so at `none` every route out is the east
# stair and west<->east crossings are inconclusive by design.
