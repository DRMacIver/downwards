# Keyhole Vault - "d" (hard): THE SEAL ANTECHAMBER.
#
# The last room before the astral seal, and the family's lid geometry worn high
# instead of dug deep. Row 5 is a single slab from wall to wall, and the top of
# the room - rows 1-4 - is a shallow processional GALLERY walked on the slab's
# back, with the ceiling door opening straight down into it. Everything below
# is the vault. The floor is split: a deep SUMP (cols 1-19, floor at row 17)
# and a raised BAY (cols 20-28, floor at row 14) that the seal door opens onto,
# with a two-tile well at cols 29-30 where you actually land coming in. Two
# floors at two heights, a lid with three holes, and a corkscrew between them:
# no chimney-chute-stairwell triptych anywhere in it.
#
# THREE HOLES IN THE LID, THREE PRICES - the family rule:
#
#   * cols 15-16, THE KEYHOLE. The ceiling mouth is four tiles wide, the slot
#     under it only two, and cols 14 and 17 of the lid are solid - so arriving
#     from above you LAND ON THE LID and dropping through is a separate,
#     deliberate act. What you land on 50px down is the ALTAR (row 11, cols
#     13-18) with the coin on it, and from the altar there is no way back: the
#     lid's underside is 50px overhead with nothing to touch, and stepping off
#     drops you 60px into the sump. Free to enter, impossible to leave by. The
#     solver's ceiling->ceiling of 199 ticks is the price of that one step: you
#     came in at the middle of the lid, and you go out the long way round.
#
#   * cols 19-22, THE SERVANTS' SLOT. The honest bare way home, and the only
#     hole that works in both directions. Under it hangs a two-rung CORKSCREW -
#     row 8 at cols 19-22 and row 11 at cols 23-26 - that zigzags down onto the
#     raised bay, three 30px rises from bay top to lid. Demanding but ungated:
#     no ability, no wall, no dash. It surfaces on the two solid lid tiles at
#     cols 17-18, one step from the ceiling mouth, which is the whole point of
#     an antechamber - the way out is visible from the moment you come in and
#     still costs three committed hops.
#
#   * cols 1-3, THE TITHE SLOT, at the far end of the sump. A 30px chimney of
#     bare facing rock between the west wall and the col-4 pillar, seven rows
#     tall, with no ledge in it anywhere. Gloves or boots only. It is the fast
#     way from the sump's treasure back to the gallery, and a bootless player
#     who came west for the coin has to walk the whole sump back east instead -
#     slowed, never trapped. Note the mirror against the rest of the family:
#     here the ability price is paid on the WEST and the free route is EAST.
#
# THE SUMP IS OPTIONAL AND EXPENSIVE. Neither crossing needs it; it is where
# two of the three coins live. Its floor is banded with two spike beds (cols
# 5-6 and 14-15) and a ward curtain at cols 9-10, with three to four clear
# tiles between every pair of threats so there is always a place to stand and
# re-read the beat. Climbing back out is the 30px cliff at col 19 onto the bay.
#
# THE BAY IS THE HELD BREATH. Eight tiles of flat stone between the seal door
# and the foot of the corkscrew, crossed by a single ward curtain at cols
# 24-25. That is the last quiet ground in the dungeon, and it is one beat wide.
