# Millrace Walk: the low underpass where the race floods on the half-beat.
#
# A pressed-flat service passage — solid stone from the ceiling down to
# row 10 — carrying west-east traffic on a broken towpath (one-ways at
# row 14) over a floodable channel floor. Two counter-phased sluice
# surges drown stretches of the channel bed (west cols 3-6, east cols
# 25-28, same 108-tick cycle half a beat apart), so the low road is a
# timed wade and the high road is dry but broken: gaps at cols 10-11
# and 20-21 drop a careless walker back into the channel, where a
# one-tile step block (cols 8-9 west, 22-23 east) is the dry re-mount
# point just beyond each surge.
#
# Mid-room, two masonry piers (cols 12-13 and 18-19) box the floor
# hatch into a proper vestibule: a one-way sill at row 16 catches
# floor-door entrants, a short bridge at row 13 spans pier-top to
# pier-top over it, and pressing down twice — through bridge, then
# sill — is the deliberate, two-stage commitment that takes you out
# the bottom. A coin sits on the sill itself; the two sluice coins
