# Switchback Spine (b / medium)
#
# One corridor folded three times. The west door opens into a low 2-tile
# tunnel (rows 15-16) that runs the full width east; at the right wall a
# two-step stair (row 15 shelf cols 27-30, row 13 shelf cols 29-30) folds up
# into the middle floor (row 11). The middle run doubles back west under a
# 20px ceiling, opening out only where it has to jump the two floor gaps;
# at the left wall a second fold (row 9 ledge cols 1-2, row 7 shelf cols
# 3-7) lifts onto the top run. The top run hops east over one-way platforms
# at cols 11-13 and two open holes, and the ceiling shaft at cols 14-17
# carries the player out.
#
# The two holes, cols 8-10 and cols 20-22, are cut clean through rows 6-14.
# Miss a hop on the top run and you do not die - you drop two floors into
# the bottom tunnel and walk the whole spine again. That soft, positional
# failure is the class identity.
#
# Medium twist, two parts:
#  1. One coin per run. The spine has to be run cleanly once instead of
#     sloppily three times, because a fall re-costs every coin still unbanked.
#  2. The chutes are two-way. Bottom floor to middle floor is 60px and middle
#     floor to top floor is 50px - both past a 40px jump, so they are gates,
#     not stairs. With dash (or a wall-jump chain) a player can climb a chute
#     straight up and skip the folds entirely; without abilities the folds
#     are the only way up and the room reads as its full three-run length.
door west
door ceiling

# Bottom run, easy. On the first stair shelf at the far east end of the
# tunnel - the end of the bottom walk and the mouth of the right-hand fold.
coin 280 140

# Middle run, easy. Hovers in the 30px floor gap at cols 20-22, taken at the
# apex of the jump across it. The ceiling is cut back to row 8 over cols
# 19-23 precisely so this jump has headroom; miss it and you drop two floors.
coin 210 90

# Top run, hard bonus. Far east of the top run, past the ceiling shaft. The
# player must clear the cols 20-22 hole, overshoot the exit, bank the coin,
# and come back west across the same hole to leave.
coin 294 34

# Shutter in the low crawl (cols 14-18, 20px clearance - walkable, but with
# no headroom to jump it). Fires at col 15 across the full crawl height,
# 90-tick cadence, 15 ticks hot: enough time to walk the 50px tunnel,
# punishing if you enter late. This is what turns the middle run's demotion
# from a free retry into a timing loss.
hazard 150 90 10 20 90 15 0
