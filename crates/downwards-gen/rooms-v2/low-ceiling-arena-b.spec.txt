# Low-Ceiling Arena "b" (medium): the two-storey car park.
# Variant axes taken: "a second lid layer to make a two-storey car park

# Two 3-tile bands are stacked - ground deck rows 13-15 over floor row 16,
# upper deck rows 9-11 over floor row 12 - and each carries a solid lid with

# storey. All vertical movement is funnelled into three punched holes: the
# west well (cols 10-11, one-way rung at row 13), the east well (cols 27-28,
# rung-free), and the exit shaft (cols 15-17, rows 3-8) that climbs to the

# The twist, made visible: the 1-tile pinch at cols 18-19 cuts the GROUND
# deck in half. It is a dash-only crawl, so without dash the east half of the
# ground floor is not adjacent to the west half at all - you reach it by
# lapping the building: west well up, run the upper deck east under its spike
# lid, drop the east well, collect, then lap all the way back. With dash the
# same gap is one ground dash. The room is short or long depending on what
# you are carrying, and the geometry that decides it is a single tile of
# headroom.
