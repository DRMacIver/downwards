# greed-loop-d -- "The Under-Hall Step", medium variant of the level class
# "Greed Loop".
#
# THE CLASS: a closed circuit. The bank sits in plain sight, the whole room is
# one lap, coins sit at increasing lap-depth, and nothing is banked until the
# lap closes back onto the bank tile.
#
# THE ROOM'S JOB IN THE DUNGEON: this is a STEP, not a tower. You come in from
# the west out of the dash-curtain hall and you are leaving through the FLOOR,
# down into the deep crossing. The mandatory route is thirteen steps east along
# a flat gutter and one press of DOWN. The room exists to put a lap between you
# and that press.
#
# THE VARIANT AXIS (what makes it not greed-loop-b): b is a donut with the lap
# climbing the right-hand chimney and returning west along the roof. This room
# is a TWO-STOREY CLOCKWISE LAP AROUND AN OFF-CENTRE CORE, and the return leg
# is the LOWER FLOOR -- the same gutter you arrived on, walked the other way.
# The core (cols 5-20, rows 5-13) is jammed against the west half of the room,
# so the lap is lopsided: a short ladder on the narrow west side, a long run
# east across the roof, and a deep, hazard-loaded descending spiral down the
# wide east well. You never climb the way you came down and you never come
# down the way you climbed; the circuit is strictly one-directional at every
# loadout because the well's last drop is 50px and cannot be reversed.
#
# THE LAP, clockwise, starting where you land:
#   0. LANDING / GUTTER (rows 15-16, cols 1-30). You arrive through the west
#      door and drop into the west shaft onto the gutter. Look east: the bank
#      is right there, four tiles of one-way slab at cols 14-17 row 16 with the
#      floor door underneath it. Stand on it, press down, you are out. This is
#      the abort, and it is visible from the first frame.
#   1. WEST LADDER (cols 1-4, rungs at rows 14 / 12 / 10 / 8 / 6, alternating
#      the col 3-4 and col 1-2 bands, every rise a comfortable 20px, and the
#      top rung stepping straight onto the core's roof at col 5). The narrow
#      side of the lopsided ring. Easy up, awkward down -- which is the point:
#      stepping onto rung three has already half-committed you.
#   2. TOP RUN (rows 1-4 over the core's roof at row 5, cols 5-20), eastward,
#      the long leg. It runs over the whole core with no floor beneath it to
#      fall back to and it dead-ends in open air at col 20.
#   3. THE WELL (cols 21-30, rows 5-16): a descending spiral of three shelves
#      hung off the core's east face and the east wall -- A (row 7, cols 21-24),
#      B (row 9, cols 27-30), C (row 12, cols 21-25). You fall off the top run
#      onto A, step east across the 20px notch onto B, step back west onto C,
#      and then off C's east lip into a 50px free-fall to the gutter at col 27.
#      Zig, zag, zig, gone. This is the deep half.
#   4. RETURN LEG (gutter, rows 15-16, westward, cols 27 -> 17). The lap closes
#      by walking back along the floor you started on, in the opposite
#      direction, up to the bank slab from its far side. Approaching the
#      treasury from the east when you left it heading east is what makes the
#      circuit read as a lap and not an out-and-back.
#
# HAZARDS ARE BACK-LOADED, all of them in leg 2 and leg 3:
#   * the shutter sits two thirds along the top run, and
#   * both spikes are past it, one at the lip of the well and one on the
#     deepest shelf.
# Legs 0, 1 and 4 -- arriving, climbing, and coming home -- are completely
# clean. Everything that can kill you is in the half of the lap you entered by
# choice, which is the shape the class wants: greed is taxed, arrival is not.
door west
door floor

# COIN 0 -- lap depth ~1/6. Hovering in the col 1-2 band of the west ladder
# between the third and fourth rungs,
# grabbed mid-climb. Its job is to prove that coins live ON the ring: it costs
# three hops and it is already 100px above the bank, so the player learns the
# geometry of the lap while collecting it, and can turn round and drop straight
# back down the shaft to cash out.
coin 12 90

# COIN 1 -- lap depth ~1/2. On the deck of the top run at cols 15-16, one tile
# short of the spike pair, and BEYOND the shutter. It is the last free thing on
# the lap: you have to beat the gate to reach it, and once you are standing on
# it the only cheap way home is back west through the gate again. Taking it and
# then looking east at the spikes is the room's decision point.
coin 152 40

# COIN 2 -- the deep one, lap depth ~3/4, and the hardest thing in the room.
# It sits in the far corner of shelf C, jammed against the core's east face at
# col 21, with the shelf's spike at col 23 between it and the only way off. You
# land on C at its east end; to take the coin you walk INTO the dead end past
# the spike, and then you must re-cross that spike carrying the whole lap's
# haul to reach the east lip and the drop home. The greedy detour is the only
# stretch of the room where you travel away from the bank with a full purse,
# and it is a dead end -- there is no bail-out from C except the way you came.
# It is also the room's ability-gated coin: bare-handed the spiral's shelves
# hand you onto C too far east to work the dead end and get back out inside the
# budget, so coin 2 reads as DASH-ONLY, exactly as the class's deep coin should.
coin 210 100

# SPIKE, cols 17-18 row 4 -- the toll at the lip of the well, and the point of
# no return. Cleared eastward it dumps you onto the two-tile perch at cols
# 19-20 or straight off into the well; cleared westward it is the price of
# aborting the lap after coin 1. Two tiles of spike with flat deck on both
# sides: a plain 20px hop, no perch precision, but it is the first thing in the
# room that can cost the run.
# SPIKE, col 23 row 11 -- the guard on shelf C, described with coin 2 above.
# It points up into open air with landing room either side, so it punishes the
# greedy walk-in and walk-out, never the arrival drop (you land east of it).

# THE SHUTTER: fills rows 3-4 at cols 12-13, a 20px-square gate across the
# lower half of the top run, leaving a 20px slot at rows 1-2 above it. Closed,
# it is a wall you leap; open, you stroll. It is the ONLY hazard on the way in
# and it sits between the ladder and everything else, so it gates the whole
# deep half in both directions -- the tax is on committing to the run and on
# backing out of it, never on arriving. 45 shut of every 160 ticks: mostly
# open, so a patient player is never blocked, and an impatient one leaps.
hazard 120 30 20 20 160 45 0
