# metronome-gallery-a -- easy variant of "Metronome Gallery".
#
# A flat west<->east corridor cut into five bays by four floor-to-ceiling
# TimedHazard curtains hanging under the lintels at cols 7, 13, 19 and 25.
# There is not a single spike in the room: the geometry is walk-and-hop trivial
# (every bay crossing is a 50-60px flat run, no gap, no rise beyond 20px), so
# the entire difficulty is WHEN, never WHERE. That inversion is the class.
#
# EASY EXPRESSION -- three deliberate choices, none of them a scale-down:
#  1. Long period, short window: 40 active of 150, so a curtain is off 73% of
#     the time. A player who simply looks before stepping is never in danger;
#     the room teaches cadence rather than punishing reflex.
#  2. The march runs WITH the player (phases 0/35/70/105 west to east), so the
#     safe opening travels east ahead of them. Walk out of bay 1 on the beat
#     and the wave carries you; the room rewards riding, not sprinting. The
#     mirror trip (east->west) runs against the wave and is the harder line the
#     completionist takes on the way back -- same room, two tempos.
#  3. Bay 1 (cols 1-6, west of the first curtain) is the classroom: flat, safe,
#     with the first coin in it, so the player banks a reward while standing
#     still and watching two full cycles of curtain 1 before committing.
#
# THE VISIBLE TWIST -- the ceiling perches. The one-way rungs at row 14 over
# bays 2 and 3 sit 20px above the floor and are the class's "slower alternate
# line": a perch is a place to spend a whole extra cycle mid-corridor rather
# than a shortcut, and bay 4 is a raised one-tile-step floor (cols 20-24, row
# 16) -- the shallow bay you arrive at already committed to the next hop.
door west
door east

# Coin 0 -- the classroom fee, in bay 1. Free, and the point: it makes the
# player stop in the one place stopping is safe, which is where the cadence is
# learned. Rock-solid on shaky (32/32) from either door.

# it, and re-read curtain 3 from the perch instead of from the floor.

# player lands, grabs and must leave inside the same window.
