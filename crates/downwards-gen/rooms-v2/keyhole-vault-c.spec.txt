# Keyhole Vault, hard variant: "the lid with three keyholes".
#
# Row 3 is the vault's lid: a solid gallery floor pierced by exactly three
# holes, and every one of them is a different price.
#   * cols 15-16  THE KEYHOLE - the ceiling door's own 20px slot. You fall
#     through it and you cannot come back up it: 60px below hangs a landing
#     pad (row 10, cols 15-17) inside a chute walled at cols 11 and 20, and
#     from that pad the lid is a full jump-and-a-half out of reach. Entering
#     is a decision; the room you have to leave is not the room you entered.
#   * cols 1-3    THE CHIMNEY - 100px of bare facing walls 30px apart with no
#     ledge anywhere in it. Boots-and-gloves only; a bootless player can fall
#     down it but never climb it. Two counter-phased shutters band it at rows
#     6 and 10, so the shortcut is also a timed commitment - there is nowhere
#     in the chimney to stop and wait for the beat.
#   * cols 21-24  THE STAIRWELL - the long, free way home: four hanging
#     shelves (rows 13, 10, 7, 4) spiralling up the vault's east side from a
#     step at cols 21-24, landing you on the lid four tiles from the keyhole.
#     No ability needed, but it is the whole east wall of the room.
#
# The twist you see on the way in: the landing pad is a pedestal over spikes.

# drop off the pad - you must step off LEFT (into the west half, toward the
# chimney) or RIGHT (into the east half, toward the step and the stairwell).
# The pad is the survey point, and stepping off it is where you choose your
# exit. The free coin sits on the pedestal itself, so the reward costs
# nothing and the choice costs everything.
