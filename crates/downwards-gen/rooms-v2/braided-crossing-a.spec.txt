# braided-crossing-a -- easy variant of "Braided Crossing (two-lane room)".
#
# One west<->east crossing split by a solid spine (rows 11-12, cols 4-27) into
# two lanes that both start in the west alcove and both end in the east alcove.
# Neither is a bonus branch: each is a complete solution, and both are clearable
# with NO abilities, so the easy variant makes the choice a matter of temperament
# rather than of loadout.
#
#   HIGH LANE -- the run along the spine top (feet row 10, 4 tiles of headroom).
#     Fast and exposed, broken by two full-depth slots at cols 11-12 and 20-21:
#     20px gaps, comfortable jumps, but a miss is not a death -- you fall
#     straight through into the gutter and finish the crossing the slow way.
#     That demotion is the braid: the lanes are the same room, not two corridors.
#
#   LOW LANE -- the gutter (rows 13-16). Slow, sightless, and safe-ish: the
#     hanging band at cols 14-17 squeezes it to 2 tiles for a walk-through
#     squeeze, and two single floor spikes at cols 8 and 23 are ordinary 20px
#     hops with the spine as a ceiling overhead.
#
# THE TWIST (variant axis "a timed hazard on the fast lane"): the right-hand
# slot carries a full-height shutter. The fast lane is not harder to execute
# than the gutter, it is harder to COMMIT to -- the read is "wait or risk"
# rather than "do you have boots", which is what keeps the easy variant from
# being a mechanical scale-down of the class.
#
# RE-CROSSING: the one-way rung at row 13, cols 11-12 sits inside the left slot,
# so a gutter player can trade up to the high lane mid-room (a full 40px jump --
# deliberately the hardest optional beat here) and a high-lane player who balks
# at the shutter can drop through the same slot. The lane-change rungs at the
# alcoves (row 15 col 3 / row 13 cols 1-2 west, mirrored east) are the two
# official on-ramps, and they double as the retreat ladder for a player bounced
# off a locked door.
door west
door east

# One coin per lane, so the completionist crosses twice, once each way.
# HIGH-LANE coin: in the vault above the spine, entered through the row-6 mouth
# at cols 15-16 off the row-8 rung. Only the high lane can see it, and it sits
# west of the shutter so the vault trip and the shutter commit are separate
# decisions rather than one compound gamble.
coin 131 50
# LOW-LANE coin: inside the 2-tile squeeze, the gutter's signature moment and
# the reason the slow lane is worth running at all.
coin 151 150

# The fast lane's toll: a full-height shutter across the right-hand slot.
# Long period, short active window -- a wait-or-commit read, not a reaction test.
